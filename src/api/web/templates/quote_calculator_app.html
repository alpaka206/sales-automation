How it works: Tier capacity is computed on a monthly basis (cycle dubbing minutes ÷ cycle length): Tier 1 ≈ 167 min/mo, Tier 2 ≈ 367 min/mo, Tier 3 ≈ 467 min/mo. The smallest tier that covers your total monthly usage is recommended.
Below Tier 1? Usage under ~167 min/mo can be served on the Pro subscription. Above Tier 3? Stay on Tier 3 and top up with Get Credit (add-on purchase).
Credit logic: 60 credits = 1 dubbing minute across all tiers. Service credits are a sales lever — the headline payment is locked per tier; only the effective per-minute rate moves.
